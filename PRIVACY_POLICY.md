# Live Location Based Price — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-05

Live Location Based Price uses location only to request weather, city search and location-specific market information. The app should request location permission only when the user chooses current-location detection.

The app may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). AdMob may process advertising identifiers and device information according to Google's policies and the user's consent choices. Replace the test ad unit before release and add a consent flow suitable for the countries where the app is distributed.

The app's backend may receive approximate coordinates and a city name to return weather and market data. Do not send precise location to any provider unless needed and disclosed. Do not store personal account data in the current backend.

Google Search-based rate values are indicative, may be stale or incomplete, and must be verified before use. They are not financial advice or a guarantee of a current official price.

Before publishing, replace this draft with a reviewed policy containing the legal publisher name, contact email, retention period, data deletion process, provider links and jurisdiction-specific disclosures.
