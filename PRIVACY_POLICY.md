# Privacy Policy — LocaRate

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

LocaRate ("the app", "we") shows indicative fuel, LPG, CNG, gold, silver and
weather information for a location you choose. This policy explains what the
app does with your information.

## Information we use

- **Location.** With your permission, the app reads your device location (or a
  city you search for) to show rates and weather for that area. Your location is
  sent to our server only to look up rates and weather for that place. We do not
  store a history of your locations and we do not attach them to your identity.
- **No account.** The app does not require or create an account, and we do not
  ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
- **App preferences.** Your language choice is stored on your device only.
- **Advertising.** The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use
  advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy
  (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the AdMob/AdSense terms for details.
  Where required, we ask for consent before personalised ads are shown.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect or sell personal information, and we do not use your location
for advertising profiling.

## How location is shared

To fetch rates and weather, the app sends the coordinates (or city name) you
selected to our backend service, which in turn queries public weather and price
sources. Coordinates are used only to resolve the nearest city and are not
retained beyond a short-lived cache.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Your choices

- You can refuse or revoke location permission in your device settings. The app
  still works with a city you search for.
- You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Data accuracy

Rates shown are **indicative** and are provided for general information only.
They are not an offer, a quote, or financial advice, and must be verified with
the original source before any purchase or transaction.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above will change when we do.
